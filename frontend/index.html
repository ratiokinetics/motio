MOTIO

discover more →
 

Motio is a visual explorer for the internals of a language model. It groups the model's internal representations into clusters and lays them out on a grid. Pick cells and draw a path. The right hand side shows you every text where the model followed that same path. Zoom down for finer categories, chain cells to find patterns, and let the shades guide you toward the most common continuations.

For technical details, please check the codebase.
